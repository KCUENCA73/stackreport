Quick dosing guide

Protein
Most people need 1.6–2.2g per kg of bodyweight daily from all food sources combined — a shake fills the gap food doesn't cover, it isn't a replacement for meals.
Creatine
3–5g per day, taken consistently, works as well as a "loading phase." Timing relative to workouts doesn't meaningfully change results.
Omega-3
Look for 1,000mg+ combined EPA+DHA per day if you're not eating fatty fish twice a week. Take with a meal that has some fat in it to help absorption.
Multivitamins
A multivitamin covers gaps, not deficiencies — if you suspect a specific deficiency (iron, D, B12), a blood test and a targeted supplement beat a multivitamin.
