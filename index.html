Standard Dosing Guide

Protein

1.6–2.2g per kg of bodyweight daily from all sources. A shake fills the gap food doesn't cover. Timing relative to workouts matters less than daily total.

Creatine

3–5g per day, taken consistently. "Loading" (20g/day for 5 days) works no better than steady dosing. Takes 2–4 weeks to build up in your system.

Omega-3

1,000mg+ combined EPA+DHA daily if you're not eating fatty fish twice a week. Take with a meal containing fat for better absorption.

Multivitamins

A multi covers gaps, not deficiencies. If you suspect a specific deficiency (D, B12, iron), get a blood test and take a targeted supplement instead.
